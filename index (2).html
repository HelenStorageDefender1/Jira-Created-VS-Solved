How this is measured, and what to keep in mind
  • Developers each quarter are those who logged development time in Jira, plus Nagu, who is on the org chart all year: six in Q1 (with Ganesh and Prashanth Sai), four from Q2.
  • Releases are the SD2026.x release-notes pages in the SDD Confluence space, by the date each page was created.
  • Days to production run from card creation, so they include time waiting in the backlog.
  • Q4 card counts run to 25 November, so Q4 created and solved numbers cover about two months.
  • Q4 runs to 23 November only, so release counts are compared per month.
