How this is measured, and what to keep in mind
  • Developers each quarter are those who logged development time in Jira, plus Nagu, who is on the org chart all year. Time logging was lighter in 2025, so its headcount is an estimate.
  • Releases are the SD2025.x and SD2026.x release-notes pages in the SDD Confluence space, by the date each page was created.
  • Days to production run from card creation, so they include backlog time. Bulk closures of old cards inflate some quarters, such as 2025 Q3.
  • 2026 runs to 23 November (25 November for card counts), so yearly comparisons use per-month rates where totals would be unfair.
Data as of 23 November 2026 (card counts 25 November). Internal to StorageDefender.
